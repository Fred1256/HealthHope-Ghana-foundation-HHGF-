Who we are

Health, dignity and opportunity for all

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to live a healthy, dignified and productive life—regardless of their circumstances.

👁️

Our Vision

To build a healthier, empowered and inclusive Ghana where every individual and community has the opportunity to live a healthy, dignified and productive life.

🎯

Our Mission

To improve the health and well-being of vulnerable and underserved communities through health education, disease prevention, community development, psychosocial support, advocacy, capacity building and partnerships.

💚

Our Core Purpose

To contribute to healthier, more resilient communities, with particular attention to children, young people, women, persons with disabilities, older people and low-income households.

What we focus on

Our areas of work

Community-centred action, shaped by local needs and delivered with appropriate partners.

📚

Health Education & Prevention

Share reliable health information, promote healthy lifestyles and encourage early health-seeking behaviour.

🎗️

HIV/AIDS Awareness & Support

Promote HIV prevention education, stigma reduction, awareness and respectful community support.

👶

Maternal, Child & Adolescent Health

Support education and initiatives that promote the health of mothers, newborns, children and young people.

🧠

Mental Health & Psychosocial Well-being

Encourage mental health awareness, emotional well-being and access to appropriate support.

♿

Disability Inclusion & Protection

Promote dignity, accessibility, equal opportunities and the safety and rights of vulnerable people.

🌱

Community Empowerment

Support poverty reduction, skills development, livelihood initiatives and stronger local capacity.

Our commitment

Strategic objectives

We aim to build practical, sustainable responses to health and social challenges across Ghana.

✓

Deliver community health outreach, screening and education with qualified health professionals and institutions.

✓

Promote sexual and reproductive health education, especially for adolescents and young people.

✓

Support vulnerable children and families through appropriate social and community-based interventions.

✓

Advocate for the rights, protection, safety and well-being of marginalised people.

✓

Conduct community needs assessments and research to guide effective programmes.

✓

Build the capacity of community leaders, volunteers, caregivers and local organisations.

✓

Develop partnerships with government agencies, health institutions, NGOs and development partners.

✓

Mobilise resources for sustainable health and community development programmes throughout Ghana.
